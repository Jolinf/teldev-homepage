The five strategic pillars behind TELDEV's approach, as numbered cards rather than icons, so the emphasis stays on the idea.

**Anatomy.** A card per pillar: a large numeral (`h3`, `border-strong`), an `h6` title and a one-sentence `small` description.

**Motion.** Cards reveal left to right, 80ms apart, and lift on hover.

**Layout.** 1 column on mobile, 2 on tablet, 5 from `lg`.

**Placeholders.** The pillar names and sentences are drafts to confirm with TELDEV.

**Accessibility.** The numeral is `aria-hidden`; order comes from DOM order.
