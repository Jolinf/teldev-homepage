Site-wide footer — logo and tagline, a sitemap split into Company/Services, contact details, social icons, and the copyright line.

**Anatomy.** Four columns (brand, Company, Services, Contact) collapsing to one on mobile; a bottom row with copyright + Privacy link and social icons.

**Spacing.** `space-8` column gap; `space-10` above the bottom row, `space-6` padding below the divider.

**Accessibility.** Contact details are real `mailto:`/`tel:` links. Social icons are icon-only `<a>` elements with `aria-label` naming the platform ("TELDEV on LinkedIn") — never relying on the icon shape alone.
