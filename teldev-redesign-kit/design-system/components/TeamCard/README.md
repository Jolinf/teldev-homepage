A founder or team member card for the About page.

**Anatomy.** A 1:1 portrait capped at 220px, `h5` name, `small` `text-muted` role, a short bio, a LinkedIn icon-link.

**Motion.** The portrait zooms slightly on hover.

**Accessibility.** The LinkedIn link's `aria-label` names the person ("Ada Okafor on LinkedIn"), never a generic "LinkedIn".
