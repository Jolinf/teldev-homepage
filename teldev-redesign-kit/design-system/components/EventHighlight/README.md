A dated card for a partnership or community event, used on the Partnerships page for UNILAG, NAMS and future events.

**Variants.** Default: a date chip (`primary` month band over a bold day number) beside a tone `Badge`, `h5` title and `small` description — for lists. `media`: a 16:9 event photo with the date chip floating over its top-left corner, text below — for highlight grids.

**Props.** `date` (`{ mon, day }`), `tag`, `title`, `description`, `media`, `note` (photo art direction).

**Motion.** Lifts on hover; the photo zooms slightly.

**Accessibility.** The date is real text (month + day), never an image, so it's announced and selectable.
